# Story Bank

What you've actually done, in numbers, with names and dates. `voice.md` holds
*how you sound*. This holds *what you have to say*. Every writing skill reads
it first, so drafts stop asking you for a number mid-request.

Lives at `~/.claude/linkedin/story-bank.md`, outside the bundle, so it never
gets pushed to git. Fill it with `/li-interview` (20-40 minutes) or by hand.

## 1. Timeline

Roles, internships, clubs, competitions. Dates matter: "March 2026" beats
"last year".

- (what, where, from-to, what you were actually responsible for)

## 2. Receipts

Numbers you can state without checking. The section drafts use most.

Good (format example, not your data): "cut deploy time from 22 minutes to 9,
team of four, Q2 2025"
Useless: "improved efficiency significantly"

- (number, what it measures, when, whose it is)

## 3. Shipped

Things that exist because you built them: apps, repos, dashboards, decks,
design systems, events.

- (what, your part, link if public, what it cost or returned)

## 4. Turning points

Moments you changed your mind or the plan changed under you.

- (what happened, what you believed before, what you believe now)

## 5. Scars

What went wrong and what it taught you.

- (what broke, the real cost, what you do differently now)

## 6. Positions

Things you'd defend in a room that disagreed. Note what holding them costs.

- (the claim, who disagrees, why you hold it anyway)

## 7. Recurring stories

The 3-4 stories you already tell out loud. They're tested: you know they land.

- (the story in two lines, the point it makes)

## 8. Resources I can give away

Templates, prompts, checklists, notes, repos you'd hand to anyone.

- (what, where it lives, who it helps)
