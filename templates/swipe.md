# Swipe file

Annotated patterns from creators whose posts work. `/li-hook-extract` adds
entries; `/li-post` reads them when it picks a hook.

**Store the pattern, not the post.** Keep the hook line (it's short and
attributed) and the structure, never the full text. Drafts borrow shapes,
never sentences (rules.md §7).

Lives at `~/.claude/linkedin/swipe.md`.

---

## Entry format

```
### {Creator} — {date seen} — {hook id}
- hook line: "{first line, quoted}"
- structure: {e.g. scenario → 4 numbered steps → what's scored → question}
- paragraph rhythm: {e.g. 1-line paras, one 3-line block at the turn}
- close: {question / P.S. / CTA, and what kind}
- numbers: reactions {N}, comments {N}, reposts {N} (if visible)
- why it worked: {one line}
- my version: {angle S1-S9 + the material I'd use}
```
