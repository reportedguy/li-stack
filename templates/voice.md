### 1C. What all six value creators share (this is the bar)

1. **The reader can act within 60 seconds** of finishing the post.
2. **Specific beats smart:** tool names, step counts, exact field names, real numbers.
3. **They give the whole thing away** in the post or the carousel. No "DM me for it."
4. **One idea per post.** Never a tour of everything they know.
5. **Receipts from their own life,** dated.

## 6. Sentence-level rules

- Line 1: a number, a bold claim, a scenario or a paradox. Never a question,
  never "Here's…".
- Talk like I'm explaining to a friend who builds: "you", contractions, short lines.
- Self-roast once. Hype the reader, never myself.
- **Precision test:** if a line could be on a motivation poster, cut it.
  If a step can't be done by someone reading on their phone, make it more specific.
- Use "(exact)" or "(free)" in parentheses for emphasis, max once.
- One CAPS word max per post. Emoji 0-2, never as bullets.
- Hinglish: 0-2 words per post ("bhai", "scene", "theek hai").
- Close with one specific question, or "template in the first comment (free)".
  Never "Thoughts?", never "comment {word} to get it" (LinkedIn's
  authenticity update targets comment-gates).
- 900-1,300 characters. Links in the first comment. 0-2 hashtags.

- Nothing confidential from past employers or clients unless you say so.
